Getting Here

Travel & Accommodation

Edinburgh in December is beautiful and cold — a few notes to help you plan. Full details to follow.

By Air

Edinburgh Airport (EDI) is around 25 minutes from the venue.

By Rail

Edinburgh Waverley is a short walk from Queen Street.

Taxis & Ubers

We don't recommend driving to the venue as it is in central Edinburgh. Instead, we suggest taking a taxi or Uber to and from the venue. Both are plentiful in the city, and Ubers can be booked easily through the app.
